ButterFact

Privacy Policy & Code of Conduct

Last updated September 6, 2026

ButterFact helps people evaluate real-world claims, take evidence-based action, and see what actually happened as a result. This document explains what information we collect, how we use it, the choices you have, and the standards we expect from everyone using ButterFact.

01

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password
  • Username

Profile information you choose to provide

  • Age, gender, nationality, occupation category, languages spoken, height, sexual orientation, MBTI type, zodiac sign
  • Bio and "what I'm looking for" text
  • Interests

Location information

If you enable location sharing, we use your location to show relevant nearby claims, events, and evidence.

Media

Photos and videos you upload as evidence, or as a profile photo.

Activity information

Claims you submit, actions you confirm, outcomes you report, and truth assessments you cast. Comments and discussion you post.

Device information

A device identifier and, if enabled, a push-notification token, used to keep you signed in and to deliver notifications.

02

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Operate the core ButterFact loop — showing you relevant claims, letting you propose and confirm actions, and recording outcomes.
  • Personalize what's shown to you, including how confrontational or automated the app's suggestions are, based on preferences you set.
  • Keep the platform safe — detecting abuse, spam, and fabricated evidence.
  • Communicate with you about your account and, where you've opted in, send notifications.
We do not sell your personal information.
03

Your choices and consent

You control what's shared, field by field, in Settings → Privacy:

  • Identity information (whether your username and profile are shown to others)
  • Location information
  • Role/participation visibility
  • Whether uploaded media may be processed automatically (for example, for evidence verification)

You can change any of these at any time. Some profile fields (age, gender, sexual orientation, nationality, height, occupation) can individually be hidden from other users while still being visible to you, in Settings → Account.

04

Data retention and deletion

You may request that we anonymize your account and permanently erase your personally identifying information at any time, via Settings → Account → Freeze or Delete Account. Once processed, this action cannot be undone. We handle this request in a manner consistent with applicable data protection law, including the GDPR right to erasure.

We retain the underlying claims, evidence, and outcome records that make up ButterFact's causal history even after an account is anonymized, since these form a shared factual record — but they are no longer linked to your identity.

05

Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information in transit and at rest, including encrypted connections and access controls limiting who can view your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

06

Code of conduct — community guidelines

ButterFact only works if the evidence people see is trustworthy. When using ButterFact, you agree to:

  • Submit claims, evidence, and outcome reports honestly and in good faith.
  • Never fabricate or manipulate evidence, including photos or videos submitted in support of a claim.
  • Cast truth assessments based on your genuine judgment, not to manipulate outcomes for other users.
  • Treat other users with respect, even when you disagree with their assessment of a claim.
  • Not harass, threaten, impersonate, or target other users.
  • Report content or behavior that violates these guidelines.

Violations may result in content removal, feature restrictions, or account suspension, as described in our Company Policies and Code of Conduct.

07

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you before they take effect.

08
